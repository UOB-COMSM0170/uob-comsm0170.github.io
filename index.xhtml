Unit Information

This foundational unit is designed to introduce students from diverse academic backgrounds to the discipline of artificial intelligence, that is it offers a systematic introduction to Python programming, enabling students to manage the workflow from data ingestion to model development. The unit covers both classic learning algorithms, as well as introductory concepts in neural networks and deep learning, the unit builds essential skills for solving real-world business problems such as classification and prediction. In addition, the course integrates hands-on practice with critical reflections on the ethical implications of AI, fostering both digital literacy and a sense of responsibility. As the first unit in the programme, it provides the essential technical and conceptual foundation for subsequent modules and the final project.

Assessment Details

The unit is assessed through a group coursework (60%) and an individual coursework (40%). See the Assessment Information page for deadlines, submission requirements, coursework guidance, marking criteria and FAQs.

Lab Work

The weekly labs provide hands-on experience with the concepts introduced in the lectures. Students will work with Python, Google Colab, pandas, scikit-learn and modern AI tooling to build practical skills in data analysis, machine learning and generative AI.

If you are not familiar with these tools, please use the Python Resources page for introductory and self-study materials.

Labs are formative and are designed to support both pieces of coursework. We strongly encourage all students to complete the exercises and experiment beyond the provided examples.

Using Google Colab

Most lab activities will be delivered through Google Colab notebooks, allowing students to run Python code directly in their browser without requiring local software installation.

Links to notebooks and datasets will be provided through Blackboard and the unit GitHub repository.
